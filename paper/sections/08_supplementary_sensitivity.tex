The randomisation conditions on recorded sectors and each sector's cluster-label counts. It assumes exchangeability within those strata; it does not control for every difference in size, market exposure, liquidity or other company characteristics. The results do not establish a causal effect of clustering on returns. This retrospective extension leaves the original analysis and reserved June 2026 evaluation protocol unchanged.
