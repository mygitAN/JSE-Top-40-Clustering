# Preserve the workflow; review the partition recommendation

The user values the paper-experiments notebook's EDA, tuning detail and results
reporting, but is dissatisfied with its clusters. The review therefore preserves
the notebook and investigates the partition recommendation rather than replacing
the research workflow.

The common-feature score optimum has a June-2025 cluster of 28 instruments and
four singleton clusters. June 2026 has a cluster of 23 and four singletons.
The selection rule allowed this because validity, coverage and k=8 did not
require multiple instruments per group. These partitions may isolate extremes
while giving limited detail within the largest group. Their numerical score
advantage remains real under the stated criterion; it does not establish their
suitability for the intended peer-group description.

The saved June search contains 488 eligible eight-cluster candidates with no
singleton on any June seed. A new, explicitly post hoc review ranks this cohort
with the existing joint common-feature silhouette/DB rule. Its top three
parameter sets are frozen for all-date, five-seed refits: 45 fits. No sector
labels enter selection and no equal-size constraint is imposed. Later singleton
outcomes remain reported, rather than filtering candidates on future results.

The closest alternative to the reconstructed baseline increases only t-SNE
iterations from 300 to 1000: perplexity=5, learning_rate=10, random initialisation,
2D, Euclidean input metric, average-linkage Manhattan hierarchy and k=8. It has
no singletons on any tested seed/date, with minimum group size 2 and maximum
sizes 8/7/9 across the three dates. A Ward/Euclidean alternative with
perplexity=10 also has no singletons throughout. The June-ranked first review
candidate with automatic learning rate develops later singletons. These results
are diagnostic evidence, not a promoted replacement or a new prespecified rule.

Retain EDA, parameter/trial records, both score spaces, stability diagnostics,
actual trees, membership-aware histories and qualified SHAP reporting. Review
cluster size distributions and original financial-feature profiles alongside
quality metrics when recommending a peer-group model. Geometric extremes,
singleton groups and sector disagreement remain distinct. A singleton may be a
substantive signal; it is not automatically an error or confirmed outlier.

first component loads strongly on revenue and shares, with GLN extreme in those
June-scaled fields. The corresponding share/capitalisation basis is unverified.
This supports checking definitions and reconstruction effects before assigning
an economic explanation. Neither sectors nor visual t-SNE separation establish
ground truth.
