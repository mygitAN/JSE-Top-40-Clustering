# ORiON checklist follow-up

The methods-to-results checklist supplied on 8 November 2026 sets the study claim
as a repeated, interpretable cross-sectional investment-screening process.
It requests additions to the methods/results bridge without certifying the inputs.

The original baseline and saved runs remain unchanged. This follow-up documents
the evidence matrix, financial units, missing-case accounting, experiment identity
and the population/observation distinction in `orion_methods_results_checklist.md`.

`orion-report-01` is a separately tracked, post hoc reporting extension using
saved `final-full-42` assignments/arrays. It introduces no model fit or tuning:

- Compare cluster median deviations from same-date population medians in the
  saved June-scaled feature space with own-class mean absolute surrogate SHAP.
  Report descriptive within-cluster feature ranks/correlations without p-values.
- Compare maximum-overlap and maximum-Jaccard Hungarian alignment on continuing
  members, using sorted labels and rejecting zero-intersection matches. Preserve
  membership events and imputation sensitivity. The baseline's existing chained
  alignment tables are not relabelled or overwritten.
- Export observation eligibility and raw-EPS/P-E diagnostics. Three-date histories
  have 39 eligible instruments; adjacent pairs have 40. Nonmembership is missing
  observation and must not become a negative screen or resolving flag.

No Hotelling statistic, F-reference formula/cutoff or temporal screening taxonomy
was inferred. The user has been asked for the missing Hotelling reference/sample,
coordinate system, centre/covariance, F calibration and level. These are required
before computing that dependent extension. A descriptive F-reference screen is
the requested scope; it must not be reported as a confirmed significance test.
Temporal flag definitions/windows and a fair nonmeaningful-P/E sensitivity
protocol also remain explicit methodological decisions.

Archived shares/capitalization, sector history and daily market-source validation
remain the submission-grade evidence blocker. Source flags, raw values, existing
baseline configurations, result promotion pointer and repository visibility are
unchanged. See the checklist for direct NIST references on distinct T² setups.
